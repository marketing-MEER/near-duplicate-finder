The weekly pipeline review

A pipeline full of old deals hides the ones that can still close. Block one afternoon, open the board and go through every deal older than sixty days. For each one, decide: is there a next step with a date? If not, either book that step now or close the deal as lost and write down why.

Next, check your stages. If a stage holds more than a third of all open deals, it is probably two stages, or deals are parked there because nobody knows what comes next. Split it or rename it so the next action is obvious.

After that first clean-up, keep a fixed slot every Friday afternoon. Sort the board by last activity, call or email the three oldest deals, and move anything without a reply for a month into a nurture list instead of leaving it on the board.

End the review by updating the forecast for next week and noting one thing that slowed deals down, so the team meeting on Monday starts with a fact instead of a feeling.
