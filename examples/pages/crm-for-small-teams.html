A CRM built for small teams

Most sales teams with fewer than ten people do not need a heavy enterprise system. They need one place to see every deal, every contact and every next step, without a week of setup. BrightCRM gives you a pipeline board, reminders that land in your inbox and simple reports that show where deals stall.

Setup takes about an hour. Import your contacts from a spreadsheet, drag your stages into the order you sell, and invite your team. There are no consultants, no custom fields you have to plan in advance and no training days. If something does not fit the way you work, you change it on the board in a few clicks.

Small teams lose deals because follow-ups slip. BrightCRM shows every deal that has not been touched for seven days in red, so nobody has to remember which lead went quiet. Reports compare this month with last month and show the win rate for each stage, so you know where to coach.

BrightCRM is a fictional company used for this example.
